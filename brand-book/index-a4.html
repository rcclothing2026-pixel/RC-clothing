01The Brand

The Art of Leisure

More than a clothing label — the embodiment of a lifestyle, lived in the moments away from the court.

We are not about the sweat; we are about the style. Not about the competition; about the community. We make considered essentials for the life that happens off the court — the long brunch, the coastal walk, the golden hour before dusk.

Ours is a quiet luxury: felt in the hand, never shouted from the chest. Timeless pieces for people who invest in a feeling.

02Archetype

The Sovereign

Leadership, control, and order drawn from chaos — but never the rule of others.

For Racket Club, the Sovereign is mastery over one's own life and leisure. We define our exclusive "club" by taste and quality, value heritage and tradition, and present a confident, classic, controlled image to the world.

03The Audience

The Modern Classicist

Discerning, quality-conscious, aged 20–45. Wealthy but never showy — they don't just buy clothes, they invest in a feeling.

Persona · She

Sara

Creative director in a coastal city. Weekend padel then a long brunch; galleries, indie cinema, art markets. Buys timeless, well-made pieces; quietly supports limited, sustainable production.

Persona · He

Arin

Investment advisor or tech founder, 30–40. Evening tennis, weekend padel; reads long-form, collects vintage rackets. Seeks understated status — no loud logos — and lasting quality.

04Voice & Tone

Refined. Confident. Evocative.

Refined. Elegant, measured language. Our typography does the same — chic and strong, never aggressive.

Confident. The calm authority of an expert. No need to shout; quality speaks for itself.

Evocative. We paint a picture — we sell the moment, not just the product.
