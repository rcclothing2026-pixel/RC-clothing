The Brand

The Art of Leisure

Racket Club is more than a clothing label — it is the embodiment of a lifestyle. We live in the moments away from the court, celebrating the culture, camaraderie and endless elegance of racket sports.

We are not about the sweat; we are about the style. Not about the competition; about the community. We make considered essentials for the life that happens off the court — the long brunch, the coastal walk, the golden hour before dusk.

Ours is a quiet luxury: felt in the hand, not shouted from the chest. Timeless pieces for people who invest in a feeling.

Archetype

The Sovereign

Leadership, control, and order drawn from chaos — but never the rule of others.

For Racket Club, the Sovereign is mastery over one's own life and leisure. We define our exclusive "club" by taste, quality and a shared appreciation of the finer things. We value heritage and tradition, and present a confident, classic, controlled image to the world.

The Audience

The Modern Classicist

Discerning and quality-conscious, aged 20–45. Wealthy, but never showy. They don't just buy clothes — they invest in a feeling.

Persona · She

Sara — Modern Classicist

Creative director at a small studio in a coastal city. Weekends are sacred: a morning padel with friends, then a long brunch. Culturally curious — galleries, indie cinema, art markets. She buys timeless, well-made pieces and quietly supports limited, sustainable production.

Voice & Tone

Refined. Confident. Evocative.

Refined. We use elegant, measured language. Our typography does the same — chic and strong, never aggressive.

Confident. We speak with the calm authority of an expert. No need to shout; the quality speaks for itself.

Evocative. Our language paints a picture. We sell the moment, not just the product.

Instead of

"Buy our new t-shirt."

We say

"Style for your leisure moments."

Instead of

"High-quality shorts."

We say

"Designed for the life that continues after the match."

The Mark & Logo System

An abstract racket-and-ball, reduced to its geometry: a square net, a crossbar, a ball. Confident, architectural, unmistakable at any size.

RACKET CLUB
RACKET CLUB Legends & Legacy
Clear space. Keep a protected zone around the logo on all sides, equal to the height of one blue square in the mark. Nothing intrudes.
Minimum size. Print: no smaller than 25 mm wide. Digital: no smaller than 120 px wide. Below this, use the mark alone.

Foreground the textures — piqué and terry cotton, aged wood, canvas, tonal embroidery. Oversized silhouettes. Portraits that feel like a memory of a perfect afternoon.
